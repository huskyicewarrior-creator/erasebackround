# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React, Vite and TypeScript on Vercel; Python image processing on a Raspberry Pi 5 with 4 GB RAM.

## Users

Developers who need to remove image backgrounds quickly and incorporate the results into their work.

## Product Purpose

A free, no-account still-image background remover with an accessible upload interface and a documented HTTP API.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Support paste, drag and drop, file upload, preview, PNG download and image copying. Support JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF within resource limits. Use conservative green-screen detection and a general-purpose model. Process on the Pi, with bounded public access. No pricing, subscriptions or ads at launch; ads may be considered later. Video and user accounts are outside the first release.

## Brand Commitments

The exact name is erasebackround.com. Avoid generic AI gradients. The approved direction uses warm neutral surfaces, ink-dark typography, a purposeful green accent and a cutout/checkerboard motif. Use motion and translucency with restraint.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer claims, benchmarks, operator identity or contact information have been supplied. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Put the image workflow first.
- Give immediate and honest status feedback.
- Make the developer API usable without an account.
- Preserve user agency and clearly explain limits and data handling.
